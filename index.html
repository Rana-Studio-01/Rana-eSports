This policy explains what data Rana eSports collects, why, and how it's protected — in plain language.

  • Account info: mobile number, username, real name, in-game name (IGN), Free Fire UID, role, bio, and an optional avatar.

  • Device info: device model, OS version, and a push-notification token (used for alerts and crash diagnostics).

  • Screen share — only while you're actively sharing in a call.
  • Gallery / photos — avatar and saved assets (plan-dependent).

Every permission is requested only when needed, with an on-screen explanation first. Denying one never crashes the app — it only locks that single feature.

Voice calls and screen sharing run through our real-time communication provider and are not recorded or stored on our servers.

Every account's chat, notifications, saved team-finder cards, and search history are private and enforced both in the app and on our servers. Only what you choose to make public — username, avatar, IGN, role, and bio — is visible to others via profile search.
